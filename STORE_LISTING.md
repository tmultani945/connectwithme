## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Connect Yourself is a quiet space for personal reflection — a place to write a prayer, intention, or thought of your own each day, in your own voice.

You choose who you speak to (God, the Universe, your higher self, a loved one, nothing at all), the mood you bring, and what's on your mind. Connect Yourself helps you put that into words.

A daily reflection waits for you on the home screen, no form to fill. When you want something specific, two taps gets you there — your usual setup is already remembered. Speak after me reads your reflection aloud in a voice you choose, line by line, so you can sit with each thought.

Belief-neutral, not religious.
Connect Yourself doesn't push a tradition. You choose how you address what's sacred to you. The app meets you where you are — anxious, grieving, hopeful, grateful, ready to manifest, or just looking for a quiet moment.

For someone you love.
Hold a friend or family member in light today. Connect Yourself can write a prayer for them — addressed by name — and lets you send it as a beautifully typeset card via Messages, WhatsApp, or Instagram.

Mood-aware.
Mark how you feel before each reflection. The reflection responds. Over time, your mood history builds a picture of how the practice is meeting you.

Designed quietly.
No emoji. No streaks-or-die. No daily nag notifications. Warm letterpress aesthetic, Fraunces serif type, soft photographic backdrops paired with each tone. A return mechanic that forgives missed days.

Six voices.
"Speak after me" plays your reflection aloud in your choice of voice — warm, deep, soft, neutral, accented. Two free, four with Connect Yourself Plus.

Connect Yourself Plus
• A daily reflection that varies with you
• Unlimited reflections, any time
• Long-form prayers, when you want more
• Voice readings without limits
• Multiple reminders, your way
• Priority generation, always

Cancel anytime from your Google Play account.

How your data is handled
Your saved reflections, moods, and preferences live on your device. When you generate a reflection, your inputs are sent through our service to an AI provider (Anthropic Claude for text, OpenAI for voice). We don't store the text or link it to you. An anonymous install identifier is used for rate-limiting only.

A note on AI
The reflections are generated by a large language model from your inputs. They are not medical, mental-health, or religious advice. If you're in crisis, please reach out to a professional or your local emergency line.

Questions: hello@connectyourself.app
```
